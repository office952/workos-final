## SERIF_RULE

Keep. Limit to the commercial artifact. Dropping it would make Ofertă a Cerere with denser numbers. Spreading it would flatten the family into editorial SaaS.

## NUMERIC_RULES

Romanian decimal comma. Space before EUR. Tabular lining through Mono. Units stay with the number. `1000 × 500 mm`. Windows: Plex is the current runtime face; do not introduce Inter as a second UI family.

## MONO_RULES

Object IDs, shortcuts, line values, rates, units. Not body. Lucrare may use Mono 12 for “Fișă de producție” as traveler metadata.

## MINIMUM_SET

Text styles `DL1/object-id` … `DL1/shortcut` on page 01 board `129:79`.

## WHAT_NOT_TO_SYSTEMIZE

Station 40 and artifact 36 are page-specific styles with prohibited-usage notes. Do not create a display ramp that every page must climb.

Lane A (late) proposed Exec station 44. Live R5 Exec `96:1405` / extract `130:755` measure IBM Plex Sans SemiBold 40. Keep 40. Do not invent a fourth display size to split the difference.
