# UI20-DL1 — Information grammar

Avoid card grids, fake spreadsheets, visual noise.

## INFORMATION_GRAMMAR

Facts sit in rows and tracks. The page personality decides density and which columns exist.

## NUMBER_AND_UNIT_DISPLAY

Mono for comparable numbers. Serif only for Ofertă consequence. Rates are owner-confirmed catalog amounts, not UI-invented prices.

## STATUS_CAUSE_ACTION_TRIAD

Status is a sentence or position. Cause is why it cannot proceed (`StateCause`). Action is the next valid 44px act (`ActionDock`). Never a colored pill alone.

## ROW_VS_TABLE_VS_LEDGER

| Surface | Form |
| --- | --- |
| Resources | Ledger. One line = one tarif. Head shares tracks with cells. |
| Ofertă | Commercial line. Few rows. Value dominates. |
| Atelier | Work rows by ownership, not a table of SKUs. |
| Lucrare | Compressed history + one current position. |
| Exec | One operation. Not a grid. |

## HISTORY_COMPRESSION

Past commercial stages remain as one line. Production owns the traveler. COMPLETE / COMPRESS shrink height; they do not delete identity.

## PROVENANCE_RULE

Quiet lineage. `din CER-1042`. Freeze ≠ acceptance ≠ Order. Synthetic fixture may already show downstream facts without an `Acceptă oferta` control.

## TECHNICAL_DETAIL_RULE

Materials appear when the instrument needs them. Unselected construction roles stay silent.

## WHAT_NOT_TO_COMPONENTIZE

Whole pages. Card wrappers around every row. A spreadsheet component “for all numbers.”
