# UI20-C1 — Quiet surfaces

Character: calm, clear, low theatricality, strong consequence language.

| Surface | Job | Intensity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Login (all gates) | enter / recover session | lowest |
| Guvernanță | authority limits | low |
| Stare sistem | health | low |
| Date firmă | seller identity | low |
| Owner write / frozen | permission consequence | low |

Login is a gate, not a destination. No Destinations chrome, no ActionDock, no ObjectRegister theatre. Session-expired copy: data remains in the organization; identity must be proven again.

Other gates (boot, network, auth_config_missing, unauthenticated) reuse the same floorplan with different consequence sentences. They are not five visual systems.

`/system` maps to this family. It is not a designed second dashboard.
