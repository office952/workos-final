# UI20-C1 — People

Operational framing only: who can work, what they can do, which processes they are eligible for.

```text
NOT_HR = YES
NOT_PONTAJ = YES
NOT_PAYROLL = YES
LOCAL_ADMIN_NAVIGATOR = Oameni | Calificări
```

## Surfaces

- **Oameni** — person, capability, availability. No MetricCard band.
- **Persoană** — ObjectRegister + eligibility + operator PIN context. Pontaj/payroll remain PRODUCT_HOLD.
- **Calificări** — operational capability, stable code, editable label. Not an application-permission matrix.

## Smart modularity

A two-person shop can keep a short eligibility list. A deeper skill matrix uses the same pages. Disabled attendance does not leave a dead Pontaj hole in the people workspace.

## Refuse

JourneyPosition, CommercialLine, employee-card grids, gamified availability.
